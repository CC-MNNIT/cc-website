## About SIH Internal Hackathon

Here is the tentative timeline for the **Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Internal Hackathon** at **MNNIT Allahabad**, organized by **Team CC**.

---

## Phase 1: Internal Hackathon

{% <collapse title="17 - 20 August: Team Formation & Registration"> %}

- Form a team of **6 members**, including **at least 1 female member**.
- Teams are encouraged to have members from **diverse branches/categories**.
- Registration Form: [SIH Internal Registration](https://forms.gle/TZjyodyCgM3H29cWA)
- Form submission deadline: **20 August, 11:59 PM**
- Select any problem statement (PS) related to the themes mentioned in the registration form.

{% </collapse> %}

{% <collapse title="21 - 31 August: Prototype Development"> %}

- Build a **working prototype** for the selected PS.
- Prepare the **PPT in the prescribed format**.

{% </collapse> %}

{% <collapse title="1 - 3 September: Evaluation"> %}

- Top-performing teams will be **nominated for Phase 2**.

{% </collapse> %}

---

## Phase 2: Submission by Nominated Teams

{% <collapse title="4 - 10 September: Final Submission"> %}

- Nominated teams will select any PS from the [SIH website](https://www.sih.gov.in/).
- Submit a **prototype + PPT** for the solution.

{% </collapse> %}
