## The Ultimate Algorithmic Arena Returns

**OPC (Open Programming Contest) 2026** is on the horizon.

Organized annually by the **Computer Coding Club (CCC), MNNIT Allahabad**, OPC is the signature individual competitive programming contest designed to test pure problem-solving intuition, mathematical ingenuity, and raw coding speed under intense time constraints.

---

### What to Expect

While the problem-setting team is crafting this year's contest behind closed doors, here is what coders can prepare for:

- **Original, Curated Problem Set**: Diverse algorithmic challenges ranging from greedy techniques and dynamic programming to advanced graph theory and data structures.
- **High-Intensity Speed Coding**: An adrenaline-packed contest format where every second and every submission penalty counts.
- **Campus & Open Leaderboard**: Battle against peers and seasoned competitive programmers to claim top rank and glory.
- **Post-Contest Editorials**: Comprehensive problem discussions and solution walkthroughs by the problem authors.

---

### Stay Tuned

Details regarding the official date, host platform, registration links, and contest timeline will be announced soon. 

Keep grinding your standard algorithms, sharpen your implementation speed on Codeforces and AtCoder, and get ready for the final coding battle of 2026.
