## About CodeSangam 2026

{% <alert_info> %}
**🏁 Development Sprint & Presentations Concluded!** CodeSangam 2026 has officially wrapped up. Over **730+ developers** and **220+ teams** built and demonstrated remarkable projects across all four tracks. The judging panel is currently conducting comprehensive evaluations, and final results will be declared soon!
{% </alert_info> %}

**CodeSangam 2026** is the annual flagship multi-track development fest organized by the **Computer Coding Club (CCC), MNNIT Allahabad**, where innovation meets execution. Developers, designers, and problem solvers from across the campus unite to engineer impactful, real-world solutions.

This year's edition channeled the mystical, reality-bending aesthetic of **Doctor Strange and the Mirror Dimension** — challenging builders to shatter conventional limitations, manipulate data dimensions, and weave spellbinding digital architectures.

### The 4 Development Tracks

Participants competed in teams of **1 to 3 members** across four major tracks:

#### 1. Webster (Web Development)
Webster is dedicated to crafting modern, performant web applications. Participants dove into frontend architecture, responsive design, robust server-side APIs, and real-time data synchronization.
- **Teams Competing**: 195 Teams
- **Team Size**: 1 – 3 Members
- [View Webster Problem Statement](https://drive.google.com/file/d/157tZ5X-MfVmQTELCBQ77SzYvEiyATXxc/view?usp=sharing)
- [Webster Live Leaderboard](https://codesangam.cc-club.in/leaderboard/webster)

#### 2. Droidrush (Android Development)
Droidrush challenged builders to create dynamic, high-performance mobile applications on the Android platform. From intuitive UI flows and Material Design 3 to seamless offline capabilities and background processing, teams engineered apps that elevate user experiences.
- **Teams Competing**: 30 Teams
- **Team Size**: 1 – 3 Members
- [View Droidrush Problem Statement](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1lQYPfDyKZVteoXBPBeESm1jCIbjjc-wK/view?usp=sharing)
- [Droidrush Live Leaderboard](https://codesangam.cc-club.in/leaderboard/droidrush)

#### 3. Logical Rhythm (Machine Learning & AI)
Logical Rhythm empowered participants to train intelligent models and extract actionable intelligence from complex datasets. The track tested expertise in data analysis, algorithm design, feature engineering, and deploying deep learning pipelines.
- **Teams Competing**: 142 Teams
- **Team Size**: 1 – 3 Members
- [View Logical Rhythm Problem Statement](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1lQCNaj1nuxRVK5tpn-yXf7shDlhGY-tS/view?usp=sharing)
- [Logical Rhythm Live Leaderboard](https://codesangam.cc-club.in/leaderboard/logicalrhythm)

#### 4. Softablitz (Software Development / Any Tech Stack)
Softablitz is an open software engineering event open to any technology stack. Participants were free to choose the programming languages, desktop frameworks, systems tools, or libraries that best realized their vision, shipping complete, production-ready pieces of software.
- **Teams Competing**: 87 Teams
- **Team Size**: 1 – 3 Members
- [View Softablitz Problem Statement](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1XTy5r8hh_XRLu-j64Q5jGa_sjuBCgKTS/view?usp=sharing)
- [Softablitz Live Leaderboard](https://codesangam.cc-club.in/leaderboard/softablitz)

### General Rules & Evaluation Criteria

- **Originality & Commit History**: All projects were initiated and developed during the official development sprint (21 Oct – 29 Oct 2026) with verified commit timelines.
- **Evaluation Criteria**: Innovation, architectural rigor, code quality, user experience, problem statement alignment, and presentation efficacy.
- **Stay Tuned**: Winners for each track, along with category awards, will be published here on the portal upon conclusion of the judging deliberations.
