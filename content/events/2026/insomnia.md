## About Insomnia 2026

**Insomnia 2026** — the flagship competitive programming contest of **MNNIT Allahabad** — was held on **Saturday, April 11, 2026** on **Codeforces**. Following the classic **ACM-ICPC** format, it challenged teams of up to three members to solve a curated set of algorithmic problems of increasing difficulty in an intense **3.5-hour** showdown of speed, logic, and teamwork.

The contest was **proctored**, and teams had to follow the proctoring guidelines to stay eligible for prize money.

With a total prize pool of **₹26,400**, the winnings were split across the **Top 3 Global teams**, the **Top 3 MNNIT teams**, and the **Fastest Solver of each problem**.

## What to Expect

- 🧠 **Algorithmic Challenges:** Problems ranging from brain-teasers to advanced graph theory.
- 🕒 **The Night Grind:** High-intensity coding atmosphere that lasts into the night.
- 🤝 **Team Strategy:** Work in teams of up to 3 to optimize your solutions.
- 🏆 **Grand Prizes:** Rewards for the top-performing teams.

---

## Format

1.  **Platform:** Codeforces (hosted as a gym/contest)
2.  **Duration:** 3.5 Hours
3.  **Team Size:** Up to 3 Members
4.  **Eligibility:** Open to all college students.
5.  **Moderation:** Proctored round — proctoring guidelines are required to be eligible for prizes.
6.  **Prize Pool:** ₹26,400, split between Top 3 Global teams, Top 3 MNNIT teams, and the Fastest Solver of each problem.
