## ICPC 2026 Preliminary Round Results

{% <alert_success> %}
**🎉 Outstanding Performance!** A total of **4 teams representing 12 students** from **MNNIT Allahabad** have qualified for the prestigious **ICPC 2026 Regional Contests** following an exceptional display of algorithmic problem solving in the Preliminary Round.
{% </alert_success> %}

The **International Collegiate Programming Contest (ICPC)** is the premier global competitive programming championship. Every year, thousands of collegiate coders across India battle in the intense online preliminary round for coveted regional onsite slots.

In the 2026 edition, MNNIT Allahabad coders showcased extraordinary speed, logic, and teamwork, earning spots across **Kanpur, Mathura, Amritapuri, and Chennai** regional sites.

### About the ICPC 2026 Regionals

The qualified teams will represent MNNIT Allahabad in the onsite Regional Contests:

- 🔹 **Kanpur Regionals:** [kanpur.indiaicpc.in](https://kanpur.indiaicpc.in/) — Hosted at IIT Kanpur
- 🔹 **Mathura Regionals:** [mathuraicpc.in](https://mathuraicpc.in/) — Hosted at GLA University, Mathura
- 🔹 **Amritapuri Regionals:** [amritaicpc.in](http://amritaicpc.in/) — Hosted at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham
- 🔹 **Chennai Regionals:** [icpc-iiitdm.vercel.app](https://icpc-iiitdm.vercel.app/) — Hosted at IIITDM Kancheepuram

Teams competing at the Regionals have the opportunity to qualify for the **ICPC Asia West Continent Championship** and the coveted **ICPC World Finals**.

### Online Round Registration Steps

1. **Form a team** of exactly 3 people.
2. **Sign up each team member individually** (if not done already) on the [ICPC Global Registration](https://icpc.global/) portal. After registration, ensure that each team member completes their profile on the same site.
3. **The team coach, Dr. Shashank Srivastava**, will create your team and register your team for one or a maximum of two regional sites. To do this, one team member needs to fill out this form: [Registration Form](https://forms.gle/dZ4sPsYXvvaoDEzNA)

{% <alert_warning> %}
**⏰ Deadline for filling the form was: 11:59 PM on September 7, 2026.**
{% </alert_warning> %}

### Important Guidelines

- Please **do not create a team** on the ICPC site; your coach will handle this for you.
- The team should be the **same** for all selected regionals.
- All members of the team should be **students of MNNIT Allahabad**.
- A team can register for a **maximum of two** regional sites.
