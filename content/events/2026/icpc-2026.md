## About ICPC 2026

**ICPC 2026** is here! Registrations for the **Online Qualifier Round** and the **Amritapuri, Kanpur, Chennai, and Mathura Regionals** have begun. This is your chance to represent **MNNIT Allahabad** on the biggest competitive programming stage in the world.

For more details about the online and onsite rounds:

- 🔹 **Amritapuri Regionals:** [amritaicpc.in](http://amritaicpc.in/)
- 🔹 **Kanpur Regionals:** [kanpur.indiaicpc.in](https://kanpur.indiaicpc.in/)
- 🔹 **Chennai Regionals:** [icpc-iiitdm.vercel.app](https://icpc-iiitdm.vercel.app/)
- 🔹 **Mathura Regionals:** [mathuraicpc.in](https://mathuraicpc.in/)

---

## Online Round Registration Steps

1. **Form a team** of exactly 3 people.
2. **Sign up each team member individually** (if not done already) on the [ICPC Global Registration](https://icpc.global/) portal. After registration, ensure that each team member completes their profile on the same site.
3. **The team coach, Dr. Shashank Srivastava**, will create your team and register your team for one or a maximum of two regional sites. To do this, one team member needs to fill out this form: [Registration Form](https://forms.gle/dZ4sPsYXvvaoDEzNA)

{% <alert_warning> %}
**⏰ Deadline for filling the form: 11:59 PM on September 7, 2026.**
{% </alert_warning> %}

---

## Important

- Please **do not create a team** on the ICPC site; your coach will handle this for you.
- The team should be the **same** for all selected regionals.
- All members of the team should be **students of MNNIT Allahabad**.
- A team can register for a **maximum of two** regional sites.
